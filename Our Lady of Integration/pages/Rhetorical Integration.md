- Rhetorical Integration is a new paradigm for religion.
- Rhetorical Integration takes as it's major premise that people experience [[States of Being]] based on their met or unmet [[Biological Needs]], existing along a spectrum of [[Satiation]].
- The Rhetorical Integration paradigm for religion categorizes the states of being into 3 super-categories of being, [[Philosophy]], [[Practice]], and [[Enlightenment]].
- Rhetorical Integration takes as its minor premise that the purpose of [[Religion]] can be distilled down to the goal of convincing a person that life has meaning and that life is worth living.
- With the minor premise in mind, Rhetorical Integration correlates the three states of being to the 3 [[Rhetorical Appeals]], [[Logos]], [[Ethos]], and [[Pathos]], holding that all three appeals play a critical role in the success of an appeal in the goal of convincing. Thus, one must integrate the 3 states of being into a single holistic way of life in order to be convinced that life has meaning and that life is worth living.
- Through the Rhetorical Integration paradigm for religion, one can assess how well a religion or religious practice accomplishes the goal of convincing its practitioners that life has meaning and is worth living. Additionally, one could identify aspects that are missing from religions or religious practices that could make them more effective.
